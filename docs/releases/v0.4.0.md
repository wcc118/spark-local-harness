# v0.4.0 — durable running-plan projection

This release adds a separate append-only ledger for concise operator-facing
plan state. A validated full revision records the objective, ordered steps,
public state, evidence references, timestamp, source, identity, and optional
reason for replanning. Private reasoning and chain-of-thought are prohibited.

The dashboard matches plans to session-aware workflow operations and renders
them side by side on desktop or workflow-first on narrow screens. It reports
missing, stale, malformed, conflicting, and superseded revisions explicitly and
uses the existing SSE refresh path. The surface remains GET-only and read-only.

`v0.3.0` is tagged and remains the rollback predecessor.
